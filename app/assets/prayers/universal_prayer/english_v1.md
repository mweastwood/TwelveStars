Priest: {Dear brothers and sisters, let us offer our prayers to God the almighty Father, who desires all people to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth.|universal_prayer_1}

Reader: {For the holy Church of God, that the Lord may guide and protect her throughout the world.|universal_prayer_2}
Reader: {Let us pray to the Lord.|universal_prayer_3}
People: {Lord, hear our prayer.|universal_prayer_4}

Reader: {For the leaders of nations, that they may govern with wisdom, justice, and compassion for all people.|universal_prayer_5}
Reader: {Let us pray to the Lord.|universal_prayer_6}
People: {Lord, hear our prayer.|universal_prayer_7}

Reader: {For all who are burdened by sickness, poverty, or loneliness, that they may find healing and comfort in Christ.|universal_prayer_8}
Reader: {Let us pray to the Lord.|universal_prayer_9}
People: {Lord, hear our prayer.|universal_prayer_10}

Reader: {For our community gathered here, and for our deceased brothers and sisters, that they may share in eternal peace.|universal_prayer_11}
Reader: {Let us pray to the Lord.|universal_prayer_12}
People: {Lord, hear our prayer.|universal_prayer_13}

Priest: {Almighty and ever-living God, hear the prayers of your people and grant what we ask in faith, through Christ our Lord.|universal_prayer_14}
People: {Amen.|universal_prayer_15}
